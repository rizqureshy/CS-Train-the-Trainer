What it is

A four-week Monthly Marathon of learning by doing.

You were picked because you're already AI-versed. Each week takes one capability deeper — thinking, customer intelligence, shipping, program design — and ends with something real that you built. By the finale, you own an AI Activation Program for your own sub-team. Train the trainer: you learn it, then you multiply it.

Track 1 · Week 1 · The skills you build
W1

Rewire Your Thinking

Jul 13–17

Critical Evaluation

Use AI to stress-test your ideas — not to validate them. Most people seek confirmation; you'll learn to seek the counter-argument.

Audience Intelligence

Profile the leader, the customer, the technologist — before you write a single word. Profile first, message second.

Radical Simplification

Explain complex things through analogies that clarify without becoming wrong — for any audience, any register.

Prompt Reverse-Engineering

Work backwards from great output to great input — the foundation of everyone who uses AI at a high level.

AI-Assisted Judgment

Friday, all four modes converge on one genuinely messy, real situation — navigation, not just production.

Friday deliverable · The Situation Room
Track 1 · Week 2 · The skills you build
W2

Customer Intelligence

Jul 20–24

Synthetic Data Engineering

Generate realistic datasets with trends, anomalies, and correlations engineered in — a capability most professionals have never touched.

Pattern Hunting

Point AI at data and push until it surfaces what you genuinely didn't see. You'll never look at a spreadsheet the same way.

Signal Synthesis

Wire market shifts, industry news, and competitor moves into your internal data for forward-looking projections.

Intelligence Reporting

Turn a week of data work into a four-section brief a senior leader can read, trust, and act on.

Data Storytelling

Friday: a live dashboard credible enough that a CFO asks follow-up questions instead of dismissing it.

Friday deliverable · The Live Dashboard
Track 1 · Week 3 · The skills you build
W3

From Idea to Production

Jul 27–31

Analog-First Design

Think with your hands — draw the process, then let AI vision read the drawing and give it structure and language.

No-Code Building

Turn a sketch and a brief into a working, animated, interactive application — without writing a line of code.

Ship-Ready Quality

The difference between working and deployable: perfect the experience, clean the code, package like a product.

Git & GitHub

The vocabulary of modern shipping — repos, commits, pushes, pulls — where your work will live.

Publishing

Friday: GitHub Pages, live URL. A tool you sketched by hand on Monday, shipped to the world by Friday.
