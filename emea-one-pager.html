Cadence & Outcomes

Cadence & Competition

  • Individual activities earn points → leaderboard.
  • Weekly checkpoint (15–20 min) rides the existing team meeting — no new meeting load.
  • Week 4 is team-based — one QBR deck per country, judged by Stefan.
  • Week 5 is an optional bonus — build & ship a tool; not required to compete.

How it maps to the outcomes

  • Adoption → activities + leaderboard build the habit.
  • Time saved → activities target real recurring work; capture "time saved" at checkpoints.
  • Confidence → ramp from prompts → proposals → decks; optional bonus to build & ship a tool.
